# Credits and swap-outs: Concept A, Citarasa Istana

## Client material (Rendang Pak Nil, Rumpun Selera Enterprise)

- Logo and favicon (`assets/img/brand/`): the Rumpun Selera Warisan × PakNil badge from the artwork on their Facebook page, background removed. Replace it with the vector badge when the client sends it.
- Packs (`assets/img/packs/`): the nine transparent pack renders from the rendangpaknil.com media library, trimmed. The `warisan-*` images are the new Warisan × PakNil pouch mockups (480 g and 980 g) from the same library.
- Pak Nil (`assets/img/people/paknil-wave.webp`): cut out of the website's banner with rembg.
- Photos (`assets/img/photos/`): the website banners and product photos (`range-*`, `paknil-range`, `key-visual`, `minang-*`, `dendeng-spices`), the Instagram post with the blue and white bowl (`rendang-bowl`, `minang-spices-ig`), and the MIHAS 2026 photos from the Facebook page (`mihas-*`). `rendang-daging` is the Pexels photo by Wahyu Prabowo that the client already uses on rendangpaknil.com.
- Stills (`assets/img/stills/`): frames from their own Facebook and Instagram reels (heat-and-serve, how it is made, Pak Nil at the table, Raya, camping, lunchbox, after work).
- Video (`assets/video/`): their reels from Instagram and Facebook, re-encoded for the web (H.264) with poster frames. The per-menu clips (`clip-*`) are cut from their product-by-product reel; Rendang Paru only appears there for about a second, so its clip is short.
- Copy: the story, product lines, pack instructions and the five reviews come from rendangpaknil.com and the packs.

## Icons, fonts, maps and code

- Icons: drawn for this concept on a 24 px grid; the Warisan cartouche and star anise are drawn from the badge. Social marks are simplified versions of the official Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp logos.
- Fonts, self-hosted: Fraunces, Anybody and Hanken Grotesk from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font Licence).
- Map: Leaflet 1.9 (BSD-2-Clause) with tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
- Motion: GSAP 3.13 with ScrollTrigger (free GSAP standard licence) and Lenis (MIT). QR codes: qrcode.js (MIT).

## Placeholders to replace before launch

- Proposed offers: the Raya set (any three packs, 5% off), the freezer stock-up (four packs a month, 10% off) and the kenduri and gift boxes (price on enquiry).
- Delivery: free frozen delivery above RM150 in the Klang Valley and RM15 below are proposals.
- The checkout is a demo: the DuitNow QR, FPX, e-wallet and card options confirm by themselves and nothing is charged. The order numbers and the delivery address in the checkout are samples.
- Agent portal: the account "Kedai Mak Teh", its orders and invoices, the tiers Ejen Gangsa, Perak and Emas (10, 15 and 20% off shop prices) and the reorder figures are sample data. Rumpun Selera sets the real agent terms.
- The new 480 g and 980 g Warisan pouches appear without prices until the client publishes them.
- The kenduri calculator assumes a 1 kg pack serves 4 to 8, from the brand's "one pack serves 2 to 4".
- Tanya Dapur, the chat helper, is a scripted proposal that answers as the kitchen team, never as Pak Nil.
- Halal: the site claims only the marks on the packs (Produk Keluaran Muslim and BeSS) and sends certificate questions to WhatsApp. Add the certificate once the client confirms it.
