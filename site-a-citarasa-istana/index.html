    Kisah warisan

    A mother’s rendang, kept exactly as she made it.

    As a boy, Pak Nil watched his late mother make rendang with great care: choosing the best spices, cooking it slowly until the oil separated, and making sure every mouthful was rich, thick and fragrant.

    He still follows her recipe “bulat-bulat”, and works with Asam Pedas London, the kitchen of Rumpun Selera, to bring it to more tables.

    “Rendang is not only food. It is memory, a family’s love, and a taste that brings everyone to the table.”
