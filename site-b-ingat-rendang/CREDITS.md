# Credits and swap-outs: Concept B, Ingat Rendang, Ingat Pak Nil!

## Client material (Rendang Pak Nil, Rumpun Selera Enterprise)

- Wordmark: "Rendang Pak Nil" is typeset in Ultra and Caveat Brush (the current logo is plain text). The Rumpun Selera Warisan × PakNil badge (`assets/img/brand/`) appears in the footer and on the About page, taken from the artwork on their Facebook page with the background removed; the favicon comes from the same badge.
- Packs (`assets/img/packs/`): the nine transparent pack renders from the rendangpaknil.com media library, trimmed. The `warisan-*` images are the new Warisan × PakNil pouch mockups (480 g and 980 g) from the same library.
- Pak Nil (`assets/img/people/paknil-wave.webp`): cut out of the website's banner with rembg.
- Photos (`assets/img/photos/`): the website banners and product photos (`range-*`, `paknil-range`, `key-visual`, `dendeng-spices`, `rendang-pot`), the Instagram post with the blue and white bowl (`rendang-bowl`), and the MIHAS 2026 photos from the Facebook page (`mihas-*`). `rendang-daging` is the Pexels photo by Wahyu Prabowo that the client already uses on rendangpaknil.com.
- Stills (`assets/img/stills/`): frames from their own Facebook and Instagram reels (heat-and-serve, how it is made, Pak Nil at the table, Raya, camping, lunchbox, after work).
- Video (`assets/video/`): their reels from Instagram and Facebook, re-encoded for the web (H.264) with poster frames. The per-menu clips (`clip-*`) are cut from their product-by-product reel; Rendang Paru only appears there for about a second, so its clip is short.
- Copy: the story, product lines, pack instructions and the five reviews come from rendangpaknil.com and the packs.

## Icons, fonts, maps and code

- Icons: [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (ISC licence), plus a wok, chilli, bowl and ketupat drawn on the same grid; brand marks from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) (CC0).
- Fonts, self-hosted from Google Fonts: Figtree and Caveat Brush (SIL Open Font Licence) and Ultra (Apache Licence 2.0).
- Map: the Google Maps embed for the Selayang address.
- Motion: GSAP 3.13 with ScrollTrigger and SplitText (free GSAP standard licence) and Lenis (MIT). The pestle thuds and pops are synthesised in the browser; there are no audio files.

## Placeholders to replace before launch

- The Set Pagi Raya (Rendang Minang 500 g and Kuah Kacang 1 kg) and its RM72 price are a proposal; the site marks the price with an asterisk.
- Delivery fees and areas are left to the team on WhatsApp; the site does not quote them.
- Agent terms are not published: the four agent roles describe who the programme suits, and the form asks the team for prices.
- The new 480 g and 980 g Warisan pouches appear without prices until the client publishes them.
- Portions: "Feeds 2 to 4" uses the brand's own line for a pack; the kenduri estimate assumes a 1 kg pack serves 4 to 8.
- Tupat the ketupat, the mascot, is our proposal and not an existing brand character.
- Halal: the site claims only the marks on the packs (Produk Keluaran Muslim and BeSS) and sends certificate questions to WhatsApp. Add the certificate once the client confirms it.
- The occasions in "Bila-bila masa" (Pagi Raya, Waktu bekal, Balik kerja, Hujung minggu, Tetamu datang) are our copy, built on their own reels and one customer review.
