Kenapa Pak Nil?

Why Pak Nil?

Because it tastes like Mak’s and it is ready before the rice. Six promises from the kitchen and the pack, explained.

Six promises

Resipi asal arwah ibu

Arwah ibu’s recipe

Every ingredient and every step still follows Pak Nil’s late mother’s recipe “bulat-bulat”, exactly as she cooked it.

Masak hingga pecah minyak

Cooked until the oil splits

The rendang is cooked slowly until the oil separates and the gravy turns thick, dry and glossy. The kitchen says the process takes a long time, on purpose.

Siap dalam 5 minit

On the table in five minutes

Thaw the pack, then heat it in the microwave or in a wok without any cooking oil. Their customers have it on the table in under five minutes.

Sejuk beku, rasa terjaga

Frozen at its best

Every pack is frozen after cooking and marked “Simpan sejukbeku” (keep frozen). It waits in your freezer until you need it.

Produk keluaran Muslim

Muslim-made

Rendang Pak Nil carries the Produk Keluaran Muslim mark and comes from the kitchen of Rumpun Selera, a restaurant and caterer since 2001. For certificate details, ask the team on WhatsApp.

Bersih, Selamat, Sihat

BeSS marked

Every pack carries the BeSS mark: Bersih, Selamat dan Sihat (clean, safe and healthy).

Dari kawah ke peti sejuk

  1. 1

    Chillies and spices

    The paste starts with heaps of red chillies and whole spices.

    Ground into rempah

    Everything is ground into the rempah right in the kitchen.

  3. 3
