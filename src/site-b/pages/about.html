A mother’s recipe, kept exactly as she made it

Bukan sekadar rendang

Rendang Minang Pak Nil is not just another dish. It is a taste handed down from Pak Nil’s late mother.

As a boy, Pak Nil watched her make rendang with great care: choosing the best spices, cooking it slowly until the oil separated, and making sure every mouthful was rich, thick and fragrant.

To this day Pak Nil keeps her recipe exactly as it was. Every ingredient and every step still follows his mother’s recipe “bulat-bulat”, so the traditional taste lives on for a new generation.

To bring this heritage to more tables at a premium standard, Pak Nil works with Asam Pedas London, the kitchen of Rumpun Selera, to produce Rendang Minang Pak Nil.

For Pak Nil, rendang is not only food. It is memory, a family’s love, and a taste that brings everyone to the table.

Yang kami pegang

What we hold on to

Four things that go into every pack that leaves Selayang.

The recipe stays as it was

Every ingredient and every step follows Pak Nil’s mother’s recipe. Nothing is changed to make it quicker or cheaper.

Cooked until the oil splits

The rendang is cooked slowly until the oil separates and the gravy turns thick, dry and glossy. The kitchen says it takes a long time, on purpose.

A real kitchen since 2001

Rumpun Selera has run a restaurant and catering kitchen since 2001, and is the team behind Asam Pedas London.

Everyone at the table

Frozen at its best, so anyone can put Mak’s rendang on the table in five minutes: Raya morning, a school lunchbox or a Tuesday night.

Dari dapur Rumpun Selera

Every pack is cooked in Rumpun Selera’s kitchen at No 2 Jalan 2/12, Bandar Baru Selayang, Batu Caves, by the team behind Asam Pedas London.
